# Evidence and Walkthrough Plan

Record a 3 to 5 minute video after completing the exercise. Show your screen and real files.

1. 0:00 to 0:30 - Explain that this is an independent practice study and identify your actual role.
2. 0:30 to 1:15 - Show the five-piece geometry, IDs, grain and finished versus pretrim dimensions.
3. 1:15 to 2:15 - Open an actual CRV job, show the CUT layer, tool choice, datum, allowance and preview.
4. 2:15 to 3:15 - Show the coupon variants and any actual test record. State clearly if no cuts have been made.
5. 3:15 to 4:15 - Demonstrate one edit, recalculate and save a new revision.
6. 4:15 to 5:00 - Show the setup sheet and explain what must be confirmed before machine-specific code release.

Do not present a colored CAD image as a VCarve machining simulation. Do not invent test photos, accepted tolerances, cycle times or production savings. A completed digital exercise is useful portfolio evidence when labeled as such.
