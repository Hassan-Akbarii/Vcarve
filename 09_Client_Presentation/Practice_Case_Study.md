## Technical approach

I used one shared master boundary for each joint, then prepared separate material-specific stock layouts. My test plan starts with a short full-scale curve segment before machining the full board. Fit allowance, finishing allowance and cutter compensation are recorded separately. The editable VCarve jobs retain the geometry and machining settings for future changes.
