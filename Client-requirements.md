First project: River No. 1 cutting board

The finished board measures 20" × 15" and combines hard maple and walnut in five adjoining pieces with flowing, complementary curves.

We need accurate, repeatable CNC files for machining these components before final assembly. The mating curves must fit consistently with minimal hand fitting.

We will provide reference images, existing drawings/templates, and details about our construction process. You will help translate these into a practical manufacturing workflow.

Scope of work

- Review our design, materials, and assembly sequence before programming.
- Create or refine the master geometry for all five components.
- Develop complementary mating curves with appropriate cutter compensation and agreed fit tolerances.
- Recommend stock allowances, tooling, workholding, and machining sequence.
- Create clearly organized VCarve Pro files with editable geometry and toolpaths.
- Account for hardwood grain direction, tear-out, part movement, and small-part retention.
- Support an initial test cut and revise the files based on the actual results.
- Document the process so we can reliably repeat it in our shop.

We are finalizing our CNC selection. Machine-specific toolpaths and posted files must be matched to the confirmed machine, controller, tooling, and workholding setup.

Deliverables

- Editable native VCarve Pro project files compatible with our software version.
- Master geometry exported in DXF or another agreed editable vector format.
- Toolpath previews and a clear setup sheet covering stock dimensions, material orientation, zero locations, tool list, machining order, and workholding.
- Recommended starting feeds, speeds, pass depths, and allowances for test cutting.
- Machine-specific posted CNC files once the equipment and setup are confirmed.
- A short recorded walkthrough explaining the files and production workflow.
- Revisions through an agreed prototype approval milestone.

Final board thickness, stock dimensions, construction sequence, and fit tolerances will be confirmed together before programming.
